36 — Week view (per-column time sort)

A single tradie's working week. Each day column is sorted asc:start_time, so jobs always appear in chronological order — drop a card anywhere in a day and it auto-reseats by time. Per-column sort also disables intra-column drag; cards can still move between days.

                Try: drag the 11:00 Friday callout to Wednesday — it'll auto-sort into the right time slot.
