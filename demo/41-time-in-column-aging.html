41 — Time-in-column aging (the "stuck quote" detector)

Each column declares data-board-column-stuck-after-days-value="N". Any card sitting there ≥ N days gets a red Nd ⚠ badge from the library. The board emits board:cardStuck once per crossing — wire it to SMS / email / Slack alerts. Drag a card into the Quoting column to reset its timer.
