50 — Optimistic in-flight pulse

Mark a card as saving with boardApi.setCardPending(id, true) and the wrapper gets a soft blue pulse animation. On failure, setCardError(id, true, msg) swaps it to a red pulse + red ring + data-card-error-msg attribute for tooltips/SR. The host clears the marker on success or dismissal. This is the JS-side counterpart of the Rails companion gem's Turbo Stream confirm/error flow.

        // click "Save…" to pulse blue; "Save (will fail)" to pulse red after 800ms
