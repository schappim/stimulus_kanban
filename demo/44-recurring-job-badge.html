44 — Recurring-job badge (↻ for annual safety checks, monthly maintenance)

Some jobs aren't one-offs — they regenerate on a schedule. A small ↻ badge on the card face says "I'll be back" so the dispatcher doesn't accidentally archive what's actually a perpetual booking. Cadence (weekly / monthly / quarterly / annual) is colour-coded and the next-due date hangs off the badge.
