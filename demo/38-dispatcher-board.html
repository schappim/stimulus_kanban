38 — Dispatcher board (unscheduled pool + day slots)

Unscheduled jobs sit in the left rail. Each tradie has a 10-day row on the right (Mon–Fri × 2 weeks). Scroll any tradie's row horizontally and the other two follow in lock-step — the day labels render once on Dave's row and the single visible scrollbar sits beneath Mia's row, so all three feel like one continuous Gantt grid. Drag a job from the rail to a slot to dispatch it; drop back on the rail to unschedule.
