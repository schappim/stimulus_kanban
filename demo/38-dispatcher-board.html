38 — Dispatcher board (ServiceM8 "Diary" pattern)

Unscheduled jobs sit in the left rail. Each tradie has a row of day-slots on the right. Drag a job from the rail to a slot to dispatch it — drop it back on the rail to unschedule. The pool is a one-column board; each tradie row is a multi-column board sharing data via boardApi.applyTransaction.
