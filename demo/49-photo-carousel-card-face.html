49 — Photo carousel on card face (4 thumbnails visible)

A 2-up + 3-stack photo strip rendered right on the card face. The first photo is the hero (wide), the next three stack vertically. A +N badge on the last tile shows extra photos when the job has more than four. Cards with no photos hide the strip entirely so they don't waste vertical space. Drag any card (including by its photo strip) between columns — the carousel re-paints in its new home via the board:rendered hook.

Tip: cards are draggable from anywhere on the card body, including the photo area. Try dragging Sewer line excavate from In progress over to Completed.
