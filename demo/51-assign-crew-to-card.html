51 — Assign crew to a card

Click any card to open the rail. Add tradies from the bottom pool, drop them with the ✕ button, or promote/demote the lead with the gold star. Built on the standard detail-rail + boardApi.applyTransaction({ update }); the card face's avatar strip re-paints on every board:rendered.
