52 — Bulk-assign crew across multiple cards

Click cards to select (or shift-click for ranges). When 2 or more are selected, the Bulk crew panel below opens with the on-N/M hint per person — pick the crew you want, then Add to all selected or Remove from all selected in one go. Lead status is preserved per card; if you remove the lead from a card, the next remaining member is promoted.
