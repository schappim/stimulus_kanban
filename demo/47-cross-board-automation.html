47 — Cross-board automation (Won quote → new job)

Two independent boards. When you drag a quote into the Won column on the left, the left board fires board:cardMoved; a single host listener calls applyTransaction({ add: [...] }) on the right board to materialise a fresh job in Scheduled. The new job flashes green for a second so the dispatcher can see what just happened.
